think with me mode

WHAT IS THINK WITH ME?

Constant reliance on AI can lead to cognitive offloading, where we let technology handle mental tasks we could otherwise handle ourselves. This mode gives you space to think, recall, and reason before AI steps in.

quick help mode

WHAT IS QUICK HELP?

Quick help is designed for when you’re in a rush and need a quick answer, not for everyday AI conversations. It helps you keep AI use intentional by setting a daily limit on the number of conversations you can have.
