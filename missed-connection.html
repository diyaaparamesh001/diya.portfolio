UX Case Study • 2026

A Missed
Connection

Aeroplane Mode made intentional disconnection effortless, but accidental activation could leave people unaware they were unreachable.

Aeroplane Mode is mainly designed to stop the phone’s wireless radios from communicating with cellular networks, including mobile networks, calls, SMS, and mobile data. But the real question is: are smartphone users aware of what happens when Aeroplane Mode is turned on?

Domain
Behavioral UX
Focus
Post-Action Awareness

The Starting Point

An INTERESTING story from
my neighbourhood that
sparked this project

01

An ordinary evening. Sarada picked up her phone for her usual call to her husband. Even while in Delhi caring for her grandchildren, she always made time to check in on the man she loved and make sure he was doing alright.

02

His phone is switched off. Maybe it is charging. Maybe he is busy. “He’ll call once he’s free.” She went back to her chores.

03

Meanwhile, back in Kerala, Madhu is trying to use his mobile data, but it isn’t working. “Ugh, has it run out already? God, I should recharge it tomorrow!”

04

“I should just sleep. I have an early train to catch.” Just like that, Madhu went to sleep.

Skip Story
05

The next morning, Madhu is leaving for his usual monthly temple visit. “Why is his phone still switched off? This is unusual. He doesn’t usually switch it off like this. Should I be worried?” She stares at her phone!

06

“Let me check!” She calls every 10–20 minutes, but the phone is still SWITCHED OFF! Anxiety slowly starts to set in. “Could something have happened to him?”

07

“Let me ask the neighbours to check the house. Maybe he went to a relative’s place? Oh my God… where is he?”

08

The neighbours rush over. They ring the bell. Knock on the door. Peek through the ventilation.

Nothing. The house is empty.

Skip Story
09

It’s already evening. “Maybe I ask the neighbours to SMASH the door? To file a MISSING COMPLAINT? Where is he?”

10

24 hours has passed since anybody had a contact with him. The neighbours were all ready to smash the door!

11

Just as they were about to force the door open, someone spotted Madhu walking towards the house with his bag.

12

He had been safe all along. His phone wasn’t switched off. Aeroplane Mode was on. He just didn’t know it was ON!
